About the role

A trusted right hand, not a task runner.

I'm an entrepreneur based in Pratumnak, Pattaya, looking for a personal assistant to support me across my personal and lifestyle needs — plus the online organization of my business.

A hands-on role mixing physical presence around Pattaya and remote work depending on what is needed. You will handle a wide range of tasks across daily logistics, travel, errands, admin, and life management. We'll set up clear processes and tools together, so you are never left guessing.

Your mission

Make my life effortless.

You organize everything and think ahead for me, so I don't have to. I don't deal with local providers, staff or offices myself: you are my single point of contact for all of it. You coordinate everyone involved, from the housekeeper to contractors, set up clear SOPs, and make sure they're followed.

To do that well, you'll need to understand exactly what I want and how I work — my preferences, my standards, my habits — so you can anticipate what I need before I ask.

What you'll do

A wide mix — never boring.

Here's a sample of what comes up. The list grows over time — what matters is that you take ownership and get things done.

  • Household & staff

    Supervising the housekeeper, writing clear SOPs for the home, and making sure they're followed day to day.

  • Research & shortlists

    Researching any topic in depth, then coming back with a short list of options that fit exactly what I'm looking for, ready for a quick decision.

  • Home & errands

    Deliveries, repairs, maintenance, dealing with contractors — quotes, follow-ups, payments. Stock checks and groceries.

  • Admin & paperwork

    Visa and 90-day reports, bank errands, paying bills, photocopies, official paperwork — everything that goes faster in Thai.

  • Health & appointments

    Booking doctors and dentists, picking up results, translating when needed, reminders.

  • Travel planning

    Researching destinations, comparing flights and hotels, booking transfers and activities, check-ins.

  • Restaurants & outings

    Reservations, finding great new spots, and keeping a list of favorites.

  • Gifts & events

    Birthday reminders, gift ideas, buying and sending on time. Full logistics for dinners and group trips.

  • Shopping & returns

    Online orders, light personal shopping, handling returns and customer service issues.

  • Online business organization

    Keeping my calendar, tasks, documents, and follow-ups organized online, in tools like Asana.
