About the role

A trusted right hand, not a task runner.

We are two entrepreneurs based in Phuket, looking for a personal assistant to support us across our personal and lifestyle needs. The role is shared between our two households, so you will be working closely with both of us.

A hands-on role mixing physical presence around Phuket and remote work depending on what is needed. You will handle a wide range of tasks across daily logistics, travel, errands, and life management. We will set up clear processes and tools with you, so you are never left guessing.

"If you're organized, proactive, and enjoy making things effortless for others — we'd love to meet you."
What you'll do

A wide mix — never boring.

Here's a sample of what comes up. The list grows over time — what matters is that you take ownership and get things done.

Home & errands

Shopping & returns

Clothing orders, light personal shopping, handling returns and customer service issues.

Health & appointments

Admin & paperwork

Who you are

The right person for this role.

Not looking for a robot. Looking for someone genuinely good at making life easier for others — and honest enough to flag what's not working.

Based in Phuket

Full-time, year-round. The role requires you to be on the island, available in person when needed.

Fluent English + Thai

Both spoken and written, at a high level. Essential for daily communication with us and local providers.

Valid driving license

Scooter or car license required. A personal vehicle is a strong plus to move around the island independently.
